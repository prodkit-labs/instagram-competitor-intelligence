# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they improve public-data analysis, reporting, documentation, examples, or provider compatibility.

Please do not contribute features for:

- private account access
- credential collection
- auto-DM or engagement automation
- fake likes, fake followers, spam, or abusive scraping
- claims of official Instagram or Meta affiliation

Good contribution ideas:

- new report templates
- better mock data
- provider adapters
- CSV and spreadsheet exports
- cost estimation helpers
- docs for responsible public-data workflows
