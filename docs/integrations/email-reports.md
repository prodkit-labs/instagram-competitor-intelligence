# Email Report Delivery Guide

Email is a simple way to deliver weekly competitor reports to clients, teams, or stakeholders.

## Best For

- weekly client reports
- internal team summaries
- lightweight recurring delivery
- agency reporting workflows

## Recommended Email Structure

```text
Subject:
Weekly Instagram Competitor Report - [Client / Brand] - [Date]

Body:
1. Short summary
2. Top 3 findings
3. Recommended actions
4. Links to full report
5. Attachments or exports
```

## Example Email

```text
Subject:
Weekly Instagram Competitor Report - Brand A - May 6

Hi team,

This week we monitored 8 public competitor accounts and collected 96 recent posts and Reels.

Top findings:

1. Reels were the strongest content format this week.
2. UGC-style product demonstrations had the highest engagement.
3. #productdemo and #ugc appeared repeatedly across top-performing content.

Recommended actions:

1. Test one UGC-style Reel next week.
2. Repurpose the top product demo format into a carousel.
3. Continue monitoring creator mentions from Brand C.

Full report:
[link]

Raw export:
[CSV link]
```

## Attachments

Recommended attachments:

```text
weekly_report.html
weekly_report.md
media_metrics.csv
hashtag_trends.csv
creator_mentions.csv
```

## Delivery Checklist

- [ ] Subject line includes client / brand name.
- [ ] Summary is short.
- [ ] Recommendations are specific.
- [ ] Full report link works.
- [ ] Attachments are included.
- [ ] No private data is included.
- [ ] Public links are verified.
- [ ] Report period is correct.
